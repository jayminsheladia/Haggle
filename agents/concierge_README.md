# Haggle — group catering in Los Angeles

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Tell Haggle what your event needs and it finds real Los Angeles caterers, collects quotes, negotiates the price down, and books one with a deposit — all in one chat.

## What it does

- Turns one sentence into a full catering request: date, time, location, headcount, budget, dietary needs and allergies
- Asks only for what's missing, with a quick form
- Finds caterers near your venue and gets quotes from them in parallel
- Negotiates within each caterer's own rules
- Takes a deposit and confirms the booking

## Example prompts

- "Need catering for 60 on Friday 6pm at Ackerman Union, $900 max, 10 vegetarian and 3 halal"
- "Catering for 30 people next Tuesday lunch in Westwood"
- "Find me a caterer for a 100-person office party in Downtown LA"
